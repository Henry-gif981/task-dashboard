1.2. Structure of MNEs

1.2.2. Organisational structures (cont.)

Table 1.2. Main organisational structures of MNEs
StructureHow it worksAdvantagesDisadvantages
International divisionAll foreign operations are grouped in one division, separate from the domestic product divisionsSimple; suitable when foreign sales are still smallDomestic divisions dominate; weak transfer of technology to foreign units
Global product divisionThe firm is organised by product line; each division manages its product worldwideStrong product focus, economies of scale and technology transferPoor coordination across products within one country; weak local responsiveness
Global area (geographic) divisionThe firm is organised by region or country; each area manages all products in its territoryHigh local responsiveness; suitable for products that need adaptationDuplication of functions; harder to achieve global scale
Global matrixManagers report to both a product head and an area headBalances global efficiency and local responsivenessDual reporting causes conflict, slow decisions and high costs

Source: Compiled by the author from Stopford and Wells (1972)
