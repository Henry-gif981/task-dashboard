Table 1.1. Classification of MNEs
TypeDescriptionTypical examples
Horizontal MNESame or similar products in several countries; serves local markets, avoids trade costsProduces the same or similar products in several countries, usually to serve local markets and avoid trade costs.Coca-Cola bottling affiliates; McDonald's; Toyota assembly plants
Vertical MNEDifferent stages of production in different countries; backward or forward; exploits cost differencesLocates different stages of production in different countries, either backward (raw materials) or forward (distribution), exploiting cost differences.Shell and Exxon (oil); Apple's electronics supply chain
Conglomerate (diversified) MNEOperates in unrelated industries across many countries, spreading risk.Samsung Group; General Electric (historically); Mitsubishi
Platform / digital MNEDelivers digital services across borders with few physical assets in each market.Google, Meta, Amazon
