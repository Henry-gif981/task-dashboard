# Issues in paper v2 for the authors

The website shows the paper's text verbatim. Items 8–10 are clear
cross-reference errors that the **website already corrects** (please fix the
Word file too); everything else is **left as in the paper** for you to decide.

## Contradictions between parts of the paper

1. **Introduction still describes three chapters.** "Objectives" lists three
   objectives matching the old plan, and "Structure of the paper" says "the paper
   consists of three chapters … Chapter 2 analyses the advantages and
   disadvantages … Chapter 3 assesses the impacts of MNEs on Viet Nam's economy".
   The body now has two chapters (Ch. 1 incl. 1.4 Advantages/disadvantages;
   Ch. 2 Impacts incl. 2.2 Viet Nam).
2. **Data period.** "Object and scope" says "using data up to the first nine
   months of 2026" and Methodology says data were "checked in October 2026",
   but the body only uses data up to the first five months of 2026 (2.2.2,
   2.2.3).
3. **Conclusion refers to removed content.** "…as the OLI paradigm explains"
   (OLI no longer appears in the body) and "structures ranging from an
   international division to a transnational network" (the transnational
   network was removed from 1.2.2 and Table 1.2). OLI is also still in the
   list of abbreviations.
4. **Paper's table of contents and list of tables (pp. 2–3) are out of date**
   (old headings 1.1.3 "Why firms become multinational", Chapter 3, old table
   titles). The website builds its own lists from the real captions.
5. **Methodology** names Hymer (1976), Dunning (1988) and Bartlett and Ghoshal
   (1989) as the theoretical basis, while the new body also relies on Porter
   (1986), Dunning (1993) and Hill and Hult (2019) (see #6).
